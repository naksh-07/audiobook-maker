# Chapter 1: The Dark Forest

The moonlight barely pierced the dense canopy of ancient pine trees. A cold northern wind swept across the mountain path, carrying the sharp scent of damp earth and coming snow.

Geralt paused near the edge of the ravine. His hand rested lightly on the silver pommel of his sword.

"Do you hear that?" he asked quietly.

From the shadows behind him, Yennefer stepped forward, her violet eyes glinting in the pale night.

"I hear only the wind, Geralt," she murmured with a slight smile. "Or have your witcher senses finally grown restless?"

"It is not the wind," Geralt replied, his voice low and steady. "Something is hunting in these woods. And it knows we are here."

# Chapter 2: The Fire at Kaer Morhen

The heavy iron-bound doors of the fortress slammed shut against the howling blizzard outside. Inside the great hall, logs of pine and oak crackled inside the stone hearth, casting long orange shadows across the vaulted stone ceiling.

Vesemir stood beside the long wooden table, carefully examining an ancient map covered in faded runes.

"You arrived later than expected," the old witcher remarked without looking up.

Geralt pulled off his snow-crusted gloves and walked toward the warmth of the fire.

"The mountain passes were treacherous," Geralt said, his boots clicking against the flagstones. "Winter has come early to the valleys."

Vesemir nodded slowly. "Then we must make our preparations quickly. The road ahead will offer no forgiveness."
