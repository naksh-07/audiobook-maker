# Chapter 1: The Alchemist's Discovery

Deep beneath the royal archives, Nicholas lit the brass oil lamp. The golden flame illuminated rows of leather-bound grimoires resting upon stone shelves.

He turned the yellowed parchment page and gasped.

"The secret of transmutation," he whispered into the silent crypt. "It was here all along."

A sudden draft extinguished the flame, plunging the chamber into darkness.
